# Source-verified empire-base cache semantics in MOO2 v1.31

This investigation follows the completed original per-farmer, per-worker, and per-scientist formula reconstruction and the resolved original three-way DATA function-pointer dispatch at `_colony_empire_base_functions`. The official original EXE (SHA-256 `4e11be14217b4aafa1839f333bf5eba037f98b0c44e9e4752c96c464c260419f`) is authenticated locally without redistribution.

Three separate original named producer bodies contain independent 10-BYTE caches. Their original DATA global key/array triples are food `0xB568/0xB56C/0x32358`, industry `0xB56D/0xB571/0x32362`, and research `0xB572/0xB576/0x3236C`. Original source `memset_` calls fill each array with **99** when colony-pointer/tag keys change or a non-null breakdown-output pointer requests component computation. Cache hits sign-extend the stored result BYTE into AX. Fresh calculations store the low BYTE while returning a WORD calculation. A fresh BYTE of 99 forces a later recomputation because it equals the sentinel.

Food uniquely bypasses the cache completely if its initial signed WORD production base is nonpositive. For selectors 0–7, the three resource-specific production modifiers are original signed player BYTEs at `+0x8A1`, `+0x8A2`, and `+0x8A3`; selector 8 receives resource-specific constants (food 6, industry 3, research 3), and selector 9 receives 4 for food and zero for industry/research. Research adds one more conditional point under an original owner/tag/flag test. These statements are about machine-decoded offsets, not inferred faction identities.

The reproducible audit in `tools/audit_colony_empire_base_cache_v131.py` checks all 823 bytes across the three named producers, original callee addresses, all direct instruction transfer targets, source-checkpoint instructions and 23 controlled cache-state cases. Its report and full annotated discussion are in `decompilation/reports/colony_empire_base_cache_v131_2026-10-16.json` and `decompilation/annotated/colony_empire_base_cache_v131.md`. The code still needs an actual original DOS runtime differential verification, a valid game-state domain proof, and a full original food climate-modifier analysis before final native equivalence can be claimed. This is not a new measurement of whole-game executable decompilation completion.
